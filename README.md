# Sales Dashboard – Power BI

## Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Sales Dashboard built in Power BI** to analyze sales performance, customers, products, marketing channels, payment methods, customer funnel behavior, and monthly sales trends.

The dashboard is designed as a portfolio project for a **Data Analyst** role and demonstrates practical use of data visualization and business analysis.

## Business Questions

- What is the overall sales performance?
- Which product categories generate the most sales?
- Which products are the top performers?
- Which marketing channels contribute the most?
- Where are customers dropping out of the purchase funnel?
- Which payment methods are most commonly used?
- Which months have the highest and lowest sales?
- Which areas/cities are included in the sales analysis?

## Key Insights

### 1. Category Performance
**Electronics** is the strongest category, with approximately **5.383M PKR** in sales. Furniture contributes around **672K PKR**, while Accessories contribute around **267K PKR**.

This indicates that the business is heavily dependent on electronics.

### 2. Product Performance
**Laptop** is the top-selling product at approximately **2.470M PKR**, followed by Smartphone at **1.495M PKR** and Monitor at **840K PKR**.

The top two products contribute a large portion of the visible product-level sales, suggesting that these products should receive strong inventory and promotional attention.

### 3. Marketing Channel Performance
Facebook is the leading channel at approximately **22.86%**, followed by Mobile App (**21.80%**) and Google Ads (**19.16%**).

The combined contribution of these three channels is approximately **63.82%**, making them the most important channels in the dashboard.

### 4. Purchase Funnel
The funnel shows:

- Visits: **500**
- Product Viewed: **405**
- Add to Cart: **265**
- Checkout: **165**
- Purchased: **112**

The overall **Visit-to-Purchase conversion rate is 22.4%**.

The largest absolute drop occurs between **Product Viewed and Add to Cart**, where **140 users** are lost. This is the most important stage to investigate for improving conversion.

### 5. Payment Methods
Bank Transfer is the largest visible payment method at **30.02%**, followed by Cash on Delivery (**23.85%**), JazzCash (**16.64%**), Card (**16.00%**) and Easypaisa (**13.45%**).

Offering and optimizing trusted local payment options can help reduce payment-stage friction.

### 6. Monthly Sales Trend
**August** is the strongest month at approximately **1.15M PKR** in the dashboard, while **September** is the lowest at approximately **0.19M PKR**.

The sharp August peak followed by a September decline is worth investigating for seasonality, promotions, inventory availability, or campaign changes.

##  Business Recommendations

1. **Improve the Product Viewed → Add to Cart stage**
   - Review product pricing and discounts.
   - Improve product descriptions and images.
   - Highlight reviews, ratings, warranty, and delivery information.
   - Test stronger call-to-action buttons.

2. **Protect the top product category**
   - Electronics is the dominant category, so inventory shortages could have a major effect on total revenue.
   - Track stock levels for high-value electronics.

3. **Focus marketing on high-performing channels**
   - Facebook, Mobile App, and Google Ads together represent about **63.82%** of the visible channel contribution.
   - Compare campaign cost against sales/revenue to identify the best ROI.

4. **Investigate the August peak**
   - Check whether August had a promotion, campaign, seasonal demand, or product launch.
   - Use the same strategy where appropriate in weaker months.

5. **Reduce checkout/payment friction**
   - Monitor failed or incomplete payments.
   - Keep popular payment options clearly visible.
   - Analyze the difference between checkout and completed purchases.
